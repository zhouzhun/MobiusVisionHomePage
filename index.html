Our Approach

Start with how people learn.

A focused practice session, a shared game world, or an immersive simulation: we choose the technology around the learner, the question, and the learning goal.

Practice with feedback

Give learners a clear next step. Our language learning work connects practice, AI feedback, and reflection so each attempt can inform the next.

Learn by doing

Make room to build, experiment, and collaborate. We design learning activities for game environments and immersive spaces around specific educational goals.

Explore new questions

Keep space for discovery. Projects such as Vovo open questions about children's interactions with AI and how technology can support literacy learning.
